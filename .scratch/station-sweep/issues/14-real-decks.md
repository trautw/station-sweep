# 14: Build 3–4 real decks

**What to build:** Design 3 to 4 original decks of about 19 × 23 tiles in portrait format, replacing the test deck in the app. They must not be modelled on any existing game. Each deck has at least one airlock pair, 2 to 4 EMP charges and a mix of drone types (Patrollers with routes, Sentinels with guarded areas, Hunters), and is designed to feel fair: no energy cells in dead ends that a Hunter can easily seal off. Add a test that loads every real deck and expects zero validation errors.

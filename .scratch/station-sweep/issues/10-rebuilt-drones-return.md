# 10: Rebuilt Patrollers and Sentinels return

**What to build:** A salvaged Patroller or Sentinel is rebuilt in the drone bay and keeps its type. It travels along the shortest path back to the nearest point of its route or back into its guarded area, then resumes its normal behaviour. On the way back it is still dangerous.

# 13: Sound and mute

**What to build:** A sound layer reacts to game events and plays simple effects generated with Web Audio, with no audio files. There are effects for collecting an energy cell, picking up an EMP charge, triggering an EMP, salvaging, being caught, clearing a deck and game over. The 🔊 button in the bottom strip mutes and unmutes sound. Audio starts only after the first user interaction.
